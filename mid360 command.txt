KEY POINTS
==========

当前 `center_attack_simple` 关键点：
- Home: `(0.8, 7.8)`
- Center2026: `(6.33, 4.32)`
- Center threshold: `0.35 m`
- HP threshold: `250`
- Heat threshold: `350`
- Attack spin velocity: `0.5 rad/s`
